Job Offer

Shipping Coordinator

Overview

Please take the time to review our formal job offer. It includes important details about your compensation, benefits and the terms and conditions of your anticipated employment with WebStore Inc.

At WebStore Inc., you'll have the opportunity to reach beyond your role and function across disciplines. WebStore Inc. offers a part-time position for you as Shipping Coordinator, reporting to Supervisor with the option to work remotely.

Each employee has $1,500 annually to spend on educational activities or material. Subscriptions and books are included in this budget, unless they are necessary for you to complete your everyday duties.

Work Process

During the first month of work, you will complete a mandatory training course under the guidance of your personal Supervisor. All required training and instructions are provided and covered by our company free of charge.

We provide necessary training over the phone, via email, online messengers, and other communication platforms.

All new employees will undergo the training course to ensure they are well prepared to begin working with us. The training process is intuitive and adaptable for all new employees, but in any case, if there are any problems, our staff will always be in touch to help you.

Duties include, but are not limited to:

  • Receiving and processing of parcels and correspondence
  • Verification of the packages according to order and invoices
  • Check the goods for damage
  • Pick-up parcels from local stores
  • Prepare packages for shipping
  • Label deliveries and allocate them to their designated place
  • Take pictures of the contents and upload the photos
  • Process all company shipments with required paperwork
  • Submit online reports and keep in contact with our department
  • Ensure invoices are signed and paid for satisfactory deliveries

Responsibilities

The Shipping Coordinator is expected to maintain accurate shipment records, communicate with relevant departments, coordinate parcel movement and ensure that required shipping documentation is completed correctly.

The position requires attention to detail, organization and timely communication throughout the shipping process.

Additional Information

This position may involve communication with customers, suppliers and internal departments. Employees are expected to follow company procedures and maintain professional communication at all times.
