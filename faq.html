Here are answers to some common questions about employment and shipping operations at WebStore Inc. If you don’t find your question here, please contact us at franklyn@lpteam.online.

1. Is this position compatible with full-time employment elsewhere?

Yes. This is a part-time role that typically requires 2–3 hours per day. It does not require presence at the company's office and can easily be combined with another job or studies.

2. Is remote work permitted?

Yes. This role is fully remote. You work from home. The only exceptions are dropping off packages at the post office and occasional retail store pickups.

3. Where is the company office located?

Our registered address is: 1025 Boxwood Rd, Wilmington, DE 19804, United States.

4. Is prior experience required?

No. Previous experience is not mandatory. All new employees go through a one-month paid training period under the guidance of a designated supervisor.

5. Is training required, and is it paid?

Yes. Training lasts one month and is fully compensated at the base salary rate ($2,500/month). During training, a supervisor walks you through every step of the process. After successful completion, you become eligible for performance bonuses starting in your second month.

6. What are the main responsibilities?

You receive assigned shipments, verify package details and condition, prepare items according to instructions, and forward them using prepaid shipping labels provided by the company.

7. How much time is required on a daily basis?

Daily tasks typically require approximately 2–3 hours, depending on workload. The schedule is flexible — you choose when to complete your tasks as long as deadlines are met.

8. What steps should I take after receiving a package?

You will receive written instructions for each shipment. Inspect the contents, photograph them, prepare a report, and ship the package using the prepaid label provided. Your supervisor will guide you through the process during training.

9. Why might a package be addressed to another individual?

Some shipments are labeled with customer or client names. You will be informed in advance and are expected to ensure availability for scheduled deliveries.

10. What if delivery occurs while I am not at home?

Where permitted by the carrier, you may authorize delivery to a specified location by completing a delivery release form in advance.

11. What happens if a delivery attempt is unsuccessful?

Carriers generally make additional delivery attempts. If further delivery is not scheduled, you may need to contact the carrier to assist with resolution:

  • FedEx: 800-463-3339
  • UPS (Domestic): 800-742-5877
  • UPS (International): 800-782-7892

12. Who is responsible for shipping costs?

The company covers all shipping expenses. Prepaid shipping labels are provided for every shipment. You never pay out of pocket.

13. How are packages forwarded?

Packages are prepared according to your instructions and delivered to authorized shipping providers such as USPS, UPS, FedEx, or other approved carriers.

14. What equipment is required?

You need reliable internet access, a cell phone, a scale, and ideally a printer. A smartphone or digital camera is needed for photographing package contents. You should also have enough storage space at home for up to 10 packages (approximately 20×20×15 inches each).

15. How is compensation provided?

Payments are issued via direct bank deposit.

16. How often are payments made?

After the training period, the base salary is paid in two equal installments per month. The bonus, if awarded, is paid once per month.

17. When can employment begin?

Work may begin after completion of the onboarding process and execution of the employment agreement.

18. What is my earning potential?

Base salary is $2,500/month. With performance bonuses, total compensation can reach up to $5,000/month. Bonuses are based on timely task completion and accurate reporting.
