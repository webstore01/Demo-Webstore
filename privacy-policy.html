At WebStore Inc, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit or make a purchase from our website.

Information We Collect

When you shop with us, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal details: name, email address, phone number, shipping and billing address.
  • Payment information: payment transactions are processed securely by trusted third-party payment providers. We do not collect, store, or have access to your full payment card details.
  • Account information: if you create an account to manage your orders.
  • Usage data: non-personal information about how visitors interact with our website, collected through analytics tools.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • Processing and fulfilling your orders.
  • Communicating with you about your orders, account, or support requests.
  • Sending promotional emails and newsletters, where you have opted in to receive such communications (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • Analyzing website usage to improve our services, including via tools like Google Analytics.
  • Ensuring security and preventing fraudulent transactions.

Third-Party Services

We may share your information with trusted third-party providers to operate our business:

  • Payment processors (e.g., PayPal, credit card gateways).
  • Email marketing services for newsletters and promotions.
  • Analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to understand how our website is used and to improve our services.

Data Security and Storage

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal and payment information. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. Payment information is handled directly by our payment providers and is not stored on our servers. Only authorized personnel have access to your information for legitimate business purposes.

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal information.
  • Opt out of marketing emails at any time via the unsubscribe link.
  • Request that we limit or stop processing your personal information for marketing purposes.

If you are a resident of the European Union, you may exercise your rights under GDPR. For any requests, contact us at franklyn@lpteam.online.

Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to ensure the proper functioning of our website, analyze traffic, and improve user experience. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page.
